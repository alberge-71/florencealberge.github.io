About me - I am a Professor at Université Paris-Saclay, conducting my research within the SATIE laboratory, where I co-lead the SICOIA group (Multi-sensor Signals, Computational Imaging, and Uncertainties in Learning) with Emanuel Aldea. My work centers on Trustworthy AI and the development of robust and calibrated models capable of leveraging imperfect data from complex physical systems, particularly applied to cultural heritage. Alongside my research, I am a faculty member at the IUT d’Orsay. I received my PhD in 1999 and my HDR in 2015.

Research Interests

Current Research Topics
  • Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence
    Reliable and robust machine learning for safety-critical applications.
  • Out-of-Distribution & Anomaly Detection
    Detection of distribution shifts, novel data, and abnormal observations in AI systems.
  • Representation Learning & Generative Models
    Structured latent representations using Variational Autoencoders and other generative approaches.
  • Uncertainty Quantification
    Confidence estimation and prediction reliability for classification and regression models.
  • Information Geometry for Machine Learning
    Geometric and statistical methods for robust learning and distribution analysis.
  • Machine Learning for Signal Processing & Communications
    AI-driven methods for signal processing, wireless communications, and intelligent sensing.
